## Overview

I designed and built a hand-sized electric vehicle using micro-robot technology. The mechanical arm was modeled in SolidWorks and laser cut; the vehicle body was drafted in AutoCAD and produced through 3D printing.

## My work

- Designed the mechanical arm in SolidWorks.
- Created the vehicle-body design in AutoCAD.
- Fabricated and assembled the laser-cut and 3D-printed components into a working prototype.

## Overview

I manufactured two types of hammers using mill, lathe, and CNC processes. The steel hammer was made by hand using the lathe and mill; a second copper hammer was CNC machined with a wooden handle.

## My work

- Produced the steel hammer using manual milling and turning operations.
- Applied CNC machining to produce the copper-hammer component.
- Worked across material preparation, machining, and finishing to create completed tools.

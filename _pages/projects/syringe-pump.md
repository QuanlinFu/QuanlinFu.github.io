## Overview

This syringe pump pairs a mechanical drive with a custom electronics enclosure. I focused on making the electrical system dependable, organized, and easy to assemble while creating a bottom housing that protects and integrates the controls.

## My contributions

- Routed and connected the system wiring for the pump electronics.
- Soldered all electrical connections to create robust, reliable joints.
- Designed the lower enclosure to organize and protect the electrical components.
- Assembled the enclosure and integrated it with the finished syringe-pump system.

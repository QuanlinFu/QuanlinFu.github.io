## Overview

I helped develop a cane-type robot built from a Raspberry Pi, electric motors, universal wheels, mechanical suspension, a camera, and carbon-fiber boards and rods. The camera identifies the position of a person’s leg and body; that position is sent to the Raspberry Pi to generate motor commands.

## My work

- Integrated the mechanical and electronic components of the robot platform.
- Used camera-based human-position detection to support robot following behavior.
- Collected data across smooth and rough travel paths.
- Improved the suspension after observing instability on rough surfaces.
